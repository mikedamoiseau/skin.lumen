[B]0.10.0[/B]
• Next episode card: in an episode's last 30 seconds a card shows the show's next episode (the next one by number, watched or not). It starts by itself a moment after the episode ends, from where you left it if you started it before. Play now skips the rest; Cancel or Back hides the card. Configure skin sets it to 30, 60 or 90 seconds, or off. When the Cinémathèque app has the next step (its Up next, "stop after this", bedtime), the card shows what the app will play instead and starts nothing. In kids mode only kids shows get one.

[B]0.9.0[/B]
• Search: every word you type must start a word of the title, in any order ("stitch lilo" finds Lilo & Stitch), also after - or ' ("man" finds Spider-Man). Text with "&" or "+" works. While typing, results come after a one-second pause instead of at every key. Kids mode's search hint no longer mentions actors.
• Kids mode: the Discover row is in random order again (it came A to Z).
• Genre: a ticked genre that the picked decade has no titles in stays in the list, dimmed, so it can be unticked.

[B]0.8.0[/B]
• Genre: tick several genres at once; the grid lists the titles in any of them, and the chip names the first and how many more ("Comedy +1"). OK ticks or unticks, Done or Back closes. The list holds only the genres that have titles (in the chosen decade, or for kids).
• Kids mode gets Genre and Decade too, on the kids film and show grids. What they open keeps to kids titles, and films are listed without collections.

[B]0.7.0[/B]
• Quick filters: Genre and Decade chips beside All and Unwatched on the Movies and TV Shows grids. Each opens a short list (Kodi's genres; 2020s back to 1950s and "Before 1950"); the chip then names the pick, the two combine, and Back still goes to Home. A pick with nothing in it says "Nothing matches these filters." Not in kids mode.
• Age rating: the info line on the detail page, the library and Home shows the title's rating as a badge: US ratings without "Rated " (PG-13, R, TV-MA…), others as written (FSK 12); none for "NR" or no rating.

[B]0.6.0[/B]
• Quick filters: the Movies and TV Shows grids get a row of chips above them: All or Unwatched, and sort by Title, Date added, Year or Rating, newest and best first. Pressing the chosen sort again turns the order round. Up from the top row reaches them; they use Kodi's own watched filter and sort, so the side menu agrees. A grid that Unwatched empties says so. Kids grids have them too.
• Home rows: Configure skin has a switch for each Home row, and adds up to three rows of your own from smart playlists, each with a name of your choice, at the end of Home. Kids mode keeps its own rows.

[B]0.5.0[/B]
• Live TV: on a channel (from a PVR add-on such as IPTV Simple) the player shows the channel's name and number, the programme when the guide has one, and "Live" in place of the times. "Ends at" gives way to the sleep timer.
• Kids mode turns live TV off: the TV and radio windows send back to Home, and a channel, radio station or recording started while kids mode is on, or playing when it comes on, stops. Nothing filters channels for children.

[B]0.4.0[/B]
• More like this: a film's detail page ends with up to three rows under the cast: more from its collection in release order, more by its director newest first, and more of its first genre best rated first. A show's page gets the genre row. Selecting a film opens its page; selecting a show opens its show page. In kids mode the rows list only kids titles.
• Studio and network logos: with Kodi's "Studio Icons - White" pack installed, the detail page's info line ends with the film's studio or the show's network logo.

[B]0.3.0[/B]
• Actor page: select someone in a film's or show's cast to see their photo and every film and show of theirs in the library, newest first, with "Director" under the films they directed. A film's cast row now starts with its director. Back from a title opened there returns to the page, and Back from the page to the film or show you started on. Not available in kids mode.
• Kids mode: a Home, Movies and TV Shows grids and search that show only titles tagged "Cinematheque Kids" (the Cinémathèque app's kids list). A smiling-face button next to power switches it on once a parent has set a PIN in Configure skin; the lock button leaves it with that PIN. Switching to kids mode stops a playing video and clears the play queue. Settings, add-ons, the long-press menu, cast, trailers and player settings are out of reach, and kids mode survives a restart.

[B]0.2.1[/B]
• Posters and thumbnails show a placeholder card while they load, so a grid no longer looks empty.

[B]0.2.0[/B]
• Search: the Search tab opens a Lumen search page and the keyboard right away. Movies and TV shows whose title matches appear while you type; confirming shows full rows that also match original titles, actors and directors.
• Show page: opening a TV show shows a "Play S2 E5" button for the next episode to watch, season tabs and that season's episodes on one screen. Selecting an episode plays it, resuming if it was started.
• Sleep timer: a moon button in the player stops playback after 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes. The time left shows next to "Ends at".
• Player: Right now moves from the playback buttons to the subtitle, settings and info buttons.

[B]0.1.2[/B]
• Home tab bar now starts at the content margin, so it no longer runs into the clock when the time is shown as 12-hour (e.g. "10:58 PM").

[B]0.1.1[/B]
• Power button at the end of the Home tab bar: opens Kodi's power menu (Exit, Power off, Reboot… depending on the device).
